# High-Readability Formatting Rule

When generating, structuring, or updating summaries, notes, or `README.md` files (especially those extracted from long transcripts, articles, or research), you MUST prioritize high scannability and readability by default. Do not wait for the user to ask you to "improve it".

Follow these formatting constraints:
1. **Logical Blocking:** Group related information into distinct sections with clear `##` headings rather than appending paragraphs linearly.
2. **Scannability:** Use bullet points (`*` or `-`) extensively for lists of concepts. Avoid dense walls of text.
3. **Emphasis:** Use **bold text** to highlight key terms, concepts, or the core subject of a bullet point.
4. **Visual Anchors:** Use appropriate emojis in headings and bullet points to serve as visual anchors that break up the text and make it more dynamic (e.g., 🧠, 🛑, ⏱️).
